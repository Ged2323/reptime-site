Questions

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Reptime works fully on iPhone. The Watch app is optional and installs automatically if you have one.

Does it work with my music?

Yes. Voice cues mix over Spotify and Apple Music, and you can choose whether music dips during cues.

Is my data private?

Yes. There are no accounts and no tracking. Routines stay on your device and Health access is limited to workouts.

Which versions are supported?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later; Apple Watch running watchOS 26 or later.

Which languages does Reptime speak?

The app is available in English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, Italiano, 日本語, 한국어 and Nederlands. It follows your iPhone’s language, including the spoken cues.

I’m new — where do I start?

The first time you open Reptime a short welcome tour explains the basics. Tap the question mark on the Routines screen any time for a guide, a legend of every icon and the tour again.
