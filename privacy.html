Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2025

Reptime ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App on your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Information We Collect

Workout Data

You may choose to log workouts (sets, reps, weights, durations). This data is stored securely on your device and is not transmitted to any external server.

Health App (HealthKit)

With your permission, the App can read and write workout information to Apple Health. We never access Health data without your explicit consent.

App Usage

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (e.g., number of workouts started) to improve performance and user experience. This data cannot be used to identify you.

How We Use Information

  • To record and display your workout routines.
  • To sync workouts between iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • To save workouts into Apple Health if you grant permission.
  • To improve app functionality and user experience.

Data Storage and Security

  • Your workout data is stored locally on your device.
  • Health data is only shared with Apple Health if you enable it.
  • We do not transmit Health or workout data to external servers.

We value your trust. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not display advertising or share data for marketing purposes.

We may employ third-party services to facilitate the App or analyse usage. These providers are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

  • Revoke HealthKit permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
  • Delete workouts directly from within the App or the Health app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
